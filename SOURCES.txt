Runtime evidence for v0.1.2:
A user-supplied Cygnus 4.5.2 save dated 2200.01.06 was read locally. Its
ntt_diag_radius=0.99458 established that the exported value is a fraction,
despite the generated documentation describing a 0-100 percentage. The
main galaxy's maximum radius and galaxy_radius field independently agree
with that ratio. The save's graph also showed why radial ranking selected
a shorter outer branch. The original save and its contents are not bundled
or published with this mod.
